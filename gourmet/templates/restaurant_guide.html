➕ How to Add Menu Items

📝 Step 1: Fill Item Details

Food Name (Required)

Enter the name of your food item
Example: Margherita Pizza, Butter Chicken, California Roll

Price in ₹ (Required)

Enter the selling price
Example: 299, 199, 450

Description (Optional)

Add details about your item
Example: "Fresh tomato base with mozzarella cheese and basil"

✅ Step 2: Submit Form

Click "➕ Add Item to Menu"

The button is located below all input fields

✓ Success!

Item added to your menu

The item will appear in your menu section immediately

📋 Menu Management

🔴 Live Items Display

Your menu items appear as cards with:

  • Item Name - Bold title at top
  • Badge Status - Shows "✓ Live" or "Unavailable"
  • Description - Optional details you added
  • Price - Highlighted in red/coral color
  • Action Buttons - Edit or Remove item

Item Status Badges

✓ Available

Item is ready for customers to order

Unavailable

Item is temporarily out of stock

📦 Managing Incoming Orders

1️⃣ Order Arrives

Status: Pending

Customer placed order, waiting for restaurant to accept

2️⃣ Accept Order

Action: Change status to Accepted

You confirm order and start preparing food

3️⃣ Mark Ready

Action: Change to Out for Delivery

Food is ready, handed to delivery partner

4️⃣ Delivered

Status: Delivered

Delivery boy updated status after customer received

💡 Pro Tip

Quickly update order status by selecting from the dropdown in the "Status" column. Changes are saved instantly!
