📋 Order Journey

1️⃣ Customer Places Order

Who: Customer (e.g., customer1)

Where: Home Page → Dashboard

What: Browse restaurants & click "Order Now"

Status: Pending
Waiting for restaurant to accept

2️⃣ Customer Pays

Who: Customer

Where: Dashboard → Pending Payment

What: Click "Pay Now" & apply coupon

Coupons:
• SAVE10 (10% off)
• NEWUSER20 (20% off)
• WELCOME50 (₹50 off)

3️⃣ Restaurant Accepts

Who: Restaurant Owner (e.g., pizza_palace)

Where: Dashboard → Incoming Orders

What: See order → Change status to "Accepted"

Status: Accepted
Restaurant preparing food

4️⃣ Food Ready for Delivery

Who: Restaurant Owner

Where: Dashboard → Incoming Orders

What: Change status to "Out for Delivery"

Status: Out for Delivery
Ready to hand off to delivery

5️⃣ Delivery Boy Picks Up

Who: Delivery Partner (e.g., delivery1)

Where: Dashboard → Delivery Orders

What: See order ready for pickup

Sees: Restaurant location, Customer info
Ready to pick up and deliver

6️⃣ Order Delivered

Who: Delivery Partner

Where: Dashboard → Delivery Orders

What: Change status to "Delivered"

Status: Delivered
Order successfully delivered!

7️⃣ Customer Rates Order

Who: Customer

Where: Dashboard → Order Status → Rate Button

What: Rate restaurant & delivery (1-5 stars)

Feedback: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Rating updates restaurant average
